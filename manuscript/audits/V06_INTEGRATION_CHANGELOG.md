## The estimator and its design history (documented honestly)

TS-SRKE = the E3/M2 LTV/Joseph kernel + a per-direction supervisor over
the clipped normalized innovations of every valid row (accepted AND
gate-rejected). Detector: two-time-scale EWMA mean-shift (fast 1/16,
slow 1/128, threshold 2.5, clip 6, warm-up/hold-off 32 rows), constants
predeclared and shared. Design iterations exposed by the full frozen
protocol and retained in this record:
1. CUSUM kappa=0.5: false alarms from constant skew bias (low-noise
   cells), E4 ESR MAPE 1.064 vs parent 0.645.
2. kappa=1.5 + slow centering: still misfired because the residual-skew
   disturbance is PERIODIC, not constant (nominal/100 ns worst).
3. Two-time-scale mean-shift detector (Gustafsson-style fast/slow
   residual filtering): zero false alarms on all 768 static blind rows,
   all ramps, and all operating transients; abrupt settling 1.52/0.48/
   1.20 ms (C/ESR/joint) vs 64.72/never/never for the parent.
