- `SUPPLEMENTARY_NOTE_S1.tex` contains the F28379D schedule, aperture, AFE, and compute details.
- `KR_CALIBRATION_NOTE.tex` records the exact v2.1 reproduction, adoption in the recalibrated campaigns, complete ESR-result rerun, and bounded TS-D-RLS sensitivity result.
