\section{Verification Methodology}
\label{sec:protocol}

\subsection{Model and Data Hierarchy}

The evaluation separates equation checks, independent plant traces, estimator comparisons, and device-realistic acquisition:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Model A is a switched-equation \Cuk{} model for sign, timing, and rapid dynamic checks.
\item Model B is an independently constructed Simscape model; 45 traces establish current scale, edge slope, and the blind observation data.
\item The estimator comparison uses 48 stratified blind operating/health cases, four noise profiles, and four residual timing-skew levels under common O1 observations.
\item The F28379D model adds register-realizable sampling, aperture/occupancy, reference/clock effects, AFE residuals, and Monte Carlo variation; it remains a simulation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Factorial and Blind Comparisons}

The observation--estimator factorial crosses a conventional mixed observation set (O0) and the proposed direction-decoupled set (O1) with RLS (E1), Dual EKF (E2), and the structured LTV/Joseph estimator (E3). All $2\times3\times768=4608$ paired rows are retained. The final comparison evaluates \TSRLS{}, \TSKF{}, and Dual EKF under O1.

The broader baseline study includes closed-form, mixed-RLS, augmented/Dual-EKF, and \Cuk{}-adapted wavelet--KF methods. An NPC spectral method was excluded because the literature provides no unique mapping to the \Cuk{} edge/charge convention; inventing one would not reproduce the published baseline.

\subsection{Fairness Rules and Metrics}

All methods use identical blind cases, observations, seeds, initialization distributions, noise/timing realizations, and predeclared hyperparameters. Failed or saturated rows are retained, no method is retuned after blind evaluation, and paired effects use 10,000 bootstrap resamples.

Abrupt C/ESR discontinuities are estimator stress tests. The principal dynamic cases are linear and smooth C-down, ESR-up, and joint ramps lasting $0.1$, 1, 10, and 100~s. Long ramps use trace-derived O1 sufficient statistics, while $0.1$~s cases are cross-checked at every switching pair with Model A. Load, input-voltage, and duty transitions hold health constant to measure false-health response. None of these trajectories is hardware aging data.

Static endpoint errors are
\begin{equation}
e_C=100\left|\hat C_N/C-1\right|,\quad
e_R=100\left|\hat r_{C,N}/r_C-1\right|,
\end{equation}
with MAPE and p95 computed over retained rows. ``Convergence cycles'' is the first cycle starting 32 consecutive cycles with both relative errors no greater than 5\%; a miss receives the 1024-cycle horizon. Rejected cycles hold the estimate and remain in the count. This internal settling metric differs from the 1024-cycle ($20.48$-ms) external reporting cadence.

For a trajectory $y(t)$ with initial truth $y_0$, the implemented tracking metric is
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{nRMSE}_y=
\sqrt{\frac{\int(\hat y-y)^2dt}{\int(y-y_0)^2dt}}.
\end{equation}
Combined nRMSE is the unweighted mean of finite C and ESR values. Normalized lag is the largest finite C/ESR delay from zero-mean cross-correlation and 50\%-trajectory crossings, divided by trajectory duration. Missed threshold crossings are retained but are not used to select the primary estimator. Supplementary Note S3 records the complete threshold and bootstrap definitions.
