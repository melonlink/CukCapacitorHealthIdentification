\section{Switched Equations Under the Adopted Sign Convention}
\label{app:equations}

For completeness, the first-order switched equations used to verify the observation signs retain the transfer-capacitor ESR and set the other parasitics to zero. Here $v_C$ is the ideal capacitor voltage and $v_T=v_C+r_Ci_C$. With $u=1$ (main switch on),
\begin{align}
\dot i_1&=V_{\mathrm{in}}/L_1, &
\dot i_2&=(v_C-r_Ci_2-v_o)/L_2,\\
\dot v_C&=-i_2/C, &
\dot v_o&=i_2/C_o-v_o/(RC_o).
\end{align}
With $u=0$ (main switch off),
\begin{align}
\dot i_1&=(V_{\mathrm{in}}-v_C-r_Ci_1)/L_1, &
\dot i_2&=-v_o/L_2,\\
\dot v_C&=i_1/C, &
\dot v_o&=i_2/C_o-v_o/(RC_o).
\end{align}
These equations yield~\eqref{eq:ic_unified} and match the specified Model-A implementation after its optional inductor, switch, diode, and source parasitics are set to zero. The ESR signs differ between states because $i_C=+i_1$ for $u=0$ and $i_C=-i_2$ for $u=1$.
