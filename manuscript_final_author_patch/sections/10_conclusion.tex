\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper converted the normal bidirectional energy transfer of a \Cuk{} converter into two topology-synchronous capacitor-health observations. A timestamp-reconstructed switching-edge row provides ESR-dominant information, while an ESR-compensated safe-window charge row provides capacitance-dominant information. Under valid CCM gates, positive finite-window information bounds establish local C--ESR identifiability and state the operating conditions under which the separation is informative.

On the blind matrix, the primary \TSRLS{} realization achieved $0.3727\%$ mean capacitance MAPE and $0.2233\%$ mean ESR MAPE. It also provided the better aggregate ramp-tracking tradeoff and the lowest operation count among the final candidates. \TSKF{} remains an uncertainty-aware extension with better confidence calibration but retained negative dynamic results. A constrained F28379D simulation supported device-realistic acquisition feasibility with worst p95 C/ESR errors of $1.6425\%/2.6109\%$.

The main conclusion concerns observation design rather than estimator complexity. Hardware and accelerated-aging studies must still validate AFE common-mode behavior, ringing, temperature dependence, calibration transfer, and measured execution time before experimental or lifetime claims are warranted.
